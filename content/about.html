About Miggle

Miggle is a daily word ladder with one destination: MIGUEL. Every day you get a new six-letter start word, and the same puzzle as everyone else.

Why “change one letter, then shuffle”?

Classic word ladders change one letter in place. MIGUEL has no neighbours that way: no English word sits one in-place letter away from it. So Miggle lets you rearrange the letters after each swap. That opens up gate words like MINGLE, HELIUM, MUESLI and GIMLET, and turns the climb into a proper puzzle.

Inspiration

Miggle is inspired by Feetle (a ladder to FEET) and Poople, and by Lewis Carroll's 1877 Doublets, the original word ladder. It is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with any of them.
