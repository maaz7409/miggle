Privacy

Short version: Miggle doesn't collect anything about you.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and no cookies.
  • No third-party requests: fonts and word lists are served from this site.

What stays on your device

Miggle saves your game in your browser's localStorage, so a reload doesn't lose your progress. Nothing in it is sent anywhere. The keys are:

  • miggle.today: today's puzzle in progress, and hints used.
  • miggle.stats: wins, streaks and your results by steps over par.
  • miggle.unlimited and miggle.unlimitedStats: your Unlimited run and stats.
  • miggle.seenIntro: whether you've seen the How to play guide.

Miggle also uses a service worker to keep a copy of the game's files, so you can play offline. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes all of it.

Hosting

Like any website, the host that serves these files may keep standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for security and reliability. Miggle itself does not read or use them.
