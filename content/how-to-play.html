How to play Miggle

Climb from the day's start word to MIGUEL in as few steps as possible.

The rules

  1. Every step is a real six-letter word.
  2. Swap exactly one letter for a different one…
  3. …then rearrange the letters however you like.
  4. Just shuffling the letters doesn't count, and you can't use a word twice.

Par, hints and streaks

  • Par is the fewest steps using everyday words. Rare words can sometimes beat it, and you'll see fireworks if you do.
  • You get two hints a day: the first shows which letter to swap, the second the whole next word. They don't change your score, but your shared result shows a 💡.
  • A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight India time (18:30 UTC). Solve on consecutive days to build a streak. Only your first solve each day counts; after that, try again for par or better just for fun.

Unlimited

Want more? Unlimited serves endless puzzles at Easy (par 3–4), Medium (5–6) or Hard (7+). Solve at par to earn hints; on Hard you get par + 2 steps per word.
